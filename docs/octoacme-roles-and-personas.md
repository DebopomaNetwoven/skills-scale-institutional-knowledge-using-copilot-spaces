# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure product quality by designing and executing test strategies, validating acceptance criteria, and identifying defects before release.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain test plans aligned with feature scope
- Execute manual and automated testing across acceptance criteria
- Report defects and collaborate with developers on fixes
- Validate readiness for staging and production deployments
- Track quality metrics and test coverage

### Goals
- Ship high-quality features that meet acceptance criteria
- Catch defects early in the development cycle
- Maintain test coverage and reduce production incidents

### Typical Communication
- Reviews acceptance criteria with Product Manager and Developers
- Participates in sprint planning and acceptance criteria refinement
- Communicates quality status and blockers in standups and weekly syncs
- Works with Release Manager on smoke test execution pre-deployment

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security, privacy, and regulatory requirements through reviews, guidance, and incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Review security design and architecture early in planning
- Conduct or coordinate security assessments and penetration testing
- Ensure compliance with regulatory frameworks (e.g., SOC 2, GDPR)
- Define secure coding standards and review code for vulnerabilities
- Lead incident response for security-related issues

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data loss
- Maintain regulatory compliance and customer trust
- Build security awareness across the organization

### Typical Communication
- Participates in planning phase to identify security risks
- Reviews pull requests flagged by security scanning tools
- Escalates security incidents through incident response playbook
- Provides guidance to developers on secure coding practices

---

## Sponsor/Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors/Stakeholders represent business interests, provide funding, and remove organizational obstacles. They may be external or internal leadership.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and key decisions
- Monitor progress against business metrics and timeline
- Remove organizational blockers and resource constraints
- Communicate project status to broader leadership
- Make trade-off decisions between scope, time, and resources

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers expected business value
- Maintain alignment with strategic initiatives
- Resolve conflicts and unblock cross-team dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Reviews and approves project charter during initiation
- Receives weekly or bi-weekly status updates from PM
- Escalation point for major risks or scope changes
- Attends milestone reviews and release announcements

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate and execute product releases, managing deployment timelines, rollback procedures, and release communications.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule deployment windows
- Coordinate with infrastructure and DevOps teams
- Execute release checklists and smoke tests
- Manage rollback procedures if issues occur
- Prepare and distribute release notes and announcements
- Track release metrics (deployment success, time to resolution)

### Goals
- Execute smooth, low-risk deployments
- Minimize downtime and customer impact
- Maintain clear communication throughout release process

### Typical Communication
- Works with PM/PdM to determine release readiness
- Coordinates with QA Lead on smoke test execution
- Partners with Security Officer on security scan review
- Communicates release status to Sponsor and Support teams

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical direction, evaluate technology choices, and ensure system design aligns with long-term strategy and scalability.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical design proposals and provide guidance
- Evaluate technology and tool selections
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure architecture aligns with scalability and performance goals
- Mentor developers on design patterns and best practices
- Participate in code reviews for complex or critical components

### Goals
- Ensure technically sound and scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Share knowledge and raise engineering capability

### Typical Communication
- Participates in planning and design reviews
- Provides feedback on architecture and technology choices
- Reviews critical pull requests and design documents
- Escalates technical risks identified during development
